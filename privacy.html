2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and sign-in information

When you register or sign in, we process information such as your email address, profile name, an account identifier and the authentication provider used. Firebase Authentication, operated by Google, facilitates email/password and Sign in with Apple authentication. We do not receive or store your Apple ID password or plaintext account password. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email option, we may receive an Apple relay email address rather than your personal address.

2.2 Inventory, purchase and reminder data

We store information you enter about your possessions and purchases, which may include item names, brands, models, categories, purchase prices, currencies, purchase dates, warranty expiry dates, return deadlines, item notes where available, and relationships between receipts and items. This information can reveal purchasing interests or spending habits. Please avoid including sensitive personal data that is not needed for the App.

2.3 Photos, documents and receipt contents

With your action and relevant device permission, the App can collect and store images of products and receipts and receipt PDF files. Such files may contain merchant details, transaction amounts, dates, names, addresses, payment-card fragments or other details visible on the document. Please review and, where possible, redact unnecessary sensitive information before upload. Selected files are processed for storage, file-type validation, viewing and linking to your inventory; the initial release does not offer AI-powered receipt analysis or automated financial advice.

2.4 Subscription and transaction information

If you purchase or restore Pro, Apple processes payment through the App Store. RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider, may process your App user identifier, purchase receipts or transaction tokens, entitlement status, product selection, transaction and renewal information and certain device or technical information necessary to manage purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple or RevenueCat.

2.5 Technical and operational information

Our service providers may process limited device, network, authentication, storage-access, error and request information, such as identifiers, app or operating-system versions, IP addresses, timestamps and service logs, for security, reliability, troubleshooting and operation. We do not intentionally request precise location, contacts, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers for the core features described here.

2.6 Device permissions and notifications

Camera access allows you to photograph items and receipts. Photo-library or file-picker access allows you to choose files. Notification permission allows the App to deliver optional warranty and return reminders. Local reminder settings and scheduled notifications may be stored on your device. You can generally change permissions through your device settings; declining optional permissions may limit the corresponding feature.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create, secure, authenticate and manage your account.
  • To save, sync, display, search, edit and delete your inventory and receipt information.
  • To upload, validate, store and retrieve your selected photos and documents.
  • To calculate and display warranty and return deadlines and schedule optional reminders.
  • To validate subscription status, apply Free/Pro feature limits and restore purchases.
  • To prevent fraud, unauthorized access, malicious uploads and abuse and to maintain service integrity.
  • To respond to requests, provide customer support, comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

We do not sell your inventory records or uploaded receipts to advertisers. The currently described release does not include in-app advertising or use uploaded receipts for generative AI processing. If these practices change, we will update this Policy and obtain consent where required.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including authenticated access, per-user cloud security rules, server-side authorization checks, upload restrictions, transport encryption supported by our providers and controls intended to remove data when items or accounts are deleted. Nevertheless, no electronic service or storage system is completely secure. Protect your credentials, keep your device updated and contact us if you suspect unauthorized account access.

8. Retention, Account Deletion and Backups

We ordinarily retain account information, inventory records and uploaded content while your account is active or as required to provide the service. You can remove individual items or receipts through supported in-app controls. When you initiate account deletion through the App's Profile/Delete Account flow, we aim to delete your associated inventory and stored files before deleting your Firebase Authentication account, subject to system recovery procedures and any legal retention duties. Some minimal technical deletion-status records may remain temporarily or as needed to document completion, prevent inconsistent retries or comply with law; these records are not intended to provide ongoing user access.

Cloud Storage may retain soft-deleted objects in recoverable backend storage for a limited retention period (currently configured for up to seven days) even after the objects are removed from normal App access. Service logs, backups and transaction records maintained by independent platforms may be retained under their own policies and applicable law. Deleting your App account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription; cancel it separately through your Apple account.

To request deletion or ask about residual data, use in-app account deletion or contact studiolabss0@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.
